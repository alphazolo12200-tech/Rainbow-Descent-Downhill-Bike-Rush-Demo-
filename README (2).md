## 🎮 What is it?

You're on a bike at the top of a huge, brightly coloured ramp suspended high above the countryside. Ahead: steep drops, rolling crests, sweeping curves and giant kickers. Pick up speed, launch into the air, flip, land clean — and get to the bottom as fast as you can.

## ✨ Demo features

- **First-person handlebar view** with a wide-angle speed effect, wind rush and screen-edge speed lines
- **Real slope physics** — you gain speed on steep drops and lose it on climbs, so tuck, brake and read the track
- **Big kickers and air time** with **front and back flips** (double flips on the longest jumps)
- **Landing system** — land level for a **PERFECT** bonus and a speed boost; land badly and you crash
- **Curves on every level** that bank the camera and push you toward the edges
- **Edge-riding multiplier** — skim the safety nets for up to x5 points (touch them and you crash)
- **Boost pads, checkpoints and falling off the track** — leave the track in mid-air and you restart from the last checkpoint
- **Slow-motion crash replays** from a cinematic orbit camera
- **Medals (🥉🥈🥇) and awards** for clean runs, flips, air time, edge riding and perfect landings
- **Procedural music and sound effects** that build in intensity as the levels get harder
- **Unlockable camera and control options** (see below)
- **Realistic scenery** — patchwork fields, a river and road, forests, villages, snow-capped mountains and drifting clouds
- **Works offline** — one HTML file, nothing else to download

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Steer | ← / → or A / D | Hold the left / right side of the screen |
| Tuck (go faster) | ↑ / W / Space | Hold the middle of the screen |
| Brake | ↓ / S | BRAKE button |
| Front flip (in the air) | Hold tuck | Hold the middle |
| Back flip (in the air) | Hold brake | BRAKE button |
| Skip crash replay | Any key | Tap |

Land with your bike level to the track for the best bonuses.

Three coloured rivals fight you down the hardest tracks on levels 3, 5 and 6. Finish first to earn the **Race Winner** award — but they bump, chase and speed up if you pull too far ahead.

Earn a medal on every level and collect **6 awards**: Clean Run, Flip Master, Air King, Edge Rider, Sharp Landings and Race Winner. Medals and awards **unlock new ways to play**:

| Unlock | How to earn it |

| 🎥 Cinematic camera | Earn 3 awards |

### 🎵 A soundtrack that grows with you
Music changes key, tempo and layers on every level — from a calm opener to a full drum-and-lead finale.

### ❄️ A third world
Frozen valleys with ice-white fields and snow-laden forests, alongside the sunset and neon night worlds.

### 🎯 Chase every record
Per-level best times, medal tiers and award tracking keep you coming back to shave seconds and master the flips.

## 💻 Requirements

- Any modern browser with WebGL (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari — desktop or mobile)
- No installation, no internet needed after the page loads
- For tilt controls (full game): a phone with a motion sensor

## 🛠️ Run it yourself

1. Download `index.html` (the demo file).
2. Double-click it — it opens in your browser. That's all.

To host it on GitHub Pages: **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main` / `(root)`**.
